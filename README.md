# Research-Workshop-course-LLM-study
Modern Large Language Models with hundreds of billions to trillions of parameters have been able to perform similarly or outperform humans in many tasks. One of the domains in which LLMs have struggled a lot has always been social cognition, which can be tested in multiple ways, one of which being on the subdomain of Theory of Mind. Our research aims to answer the question of how different regional training and model sizes between and within LLMs affect their performance on a theory of mind test.
